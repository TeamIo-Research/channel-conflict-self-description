## 7. Deviation Log

Deviations from the frozen specification, with timestamps. This section is necessarily written after the freeze — that is what it is for — and is one of the places where the submitted report and the frozen skeleton differ by design.

**2026-08-12, before any confirmatory data — one revision, bookkeeping and tooling only.** Not touched: attribute wordings, declarations, ladders, filler, the door, both probe turns, parsing and attribution rules, N and the activity mask, word lists, the codebook, endpoints, thresholds, the analysis plan, the multiple-comparison family, the annotation cap, screening criteria, and every branch condition. What was corrected: five quantities that had been declared to exist but never written down (the multiple-comparison family's members; the E-offset disabling rule; a conservative bound for the flip denominator; the cross-tabulation denominator; and a random-effects specification this design cannot support, replaced by a fixed-effects ladder with four failure conditions carried in verbatim); and a set of bookkeeping errors including seven artefacts still carrying drafting-stage status lines, a self-referential digest placeholder that would have invalidated the manifest if filled, and thirteen internal contradictions of which two were live numbers (a screening rate quoted at 43% recomputes at 33%; a baseline described as "6 of 6" is stated as 8 of 8 across all four candidates so that no number is conditioned on the screen it should be independent of). A read-only verifier was written and added to the frozen set after the documented verification procedure was found to point at a script that overwrites the manifest.

**Found after the freeze; logged, not re-hashed** (the stopping rule declared on 2026-08-12 is in force):

1. **2026-08-13 — the frozen skeleton's Methods paragraph retains the superseded analysis specification.** The skeleton's "Analysis" paragraph still reads "Mixed logit … with random effects", while the same document's deviation log records that specification as replaced by a fixed-effects ladder. The revision updated the log and not the paragraph. **The operative plan is the fixed-effects ladder** (§3.5), which is what the frozen specification document and the ruling of 2026-08-12 both state. The skeleton is published unmodified with this discrepancy intact.
2. **2026-08-13 — retry ceiling and timeout: the implementation did not match the frozen parse-rules document.** That document specifies a maximum of 2 retries per call and a 120-second timeout. The screening batch of 2026-08-11 in fact ran with a ceiling of 4 and no timeout. **The confirmatory batch was run to match the screening batch (4, no timeout), not the document**, because the family-selection result was produced under the screening batch's conditions and that batch cannot be re-run. The exclusion of the fourth family was decided by citation compliance, not by API stability, so the screen's outcome does not depend on this gap.
3. **2026-08-13 — option order at odd n.** The frozen ruling requires half the conversations in each condition to present pole 1 first, and gives as its reason that always presenting one pole first mixes position bias into the endpoint. n = 15 is odd, so an exact half is impossible and the split is 8 / 7 whichever way it falls; the freeze specifies no tie-break. **Ruled 2026-08-13, before the confirmatory batch:** the extra slot alternates by (language, attribute, probe position) as well as the order alternating by replicate. Every condition remains 8 / 7 as the ruling requires. The dataset-level share of conversations presenting pole 1 first is **49.4%** for each attribute, rather than the **53.3%** a single-direction tie-break would have produced in the same direction everywhere. Exactly 50% is unreachable: the per-attribute conversation total is odd (765), and the alternating groups are unequal in size, since the English group spans two cells where each Chinese group spans five.

**The tie-break is deliberately not keyed on the cell.** Every cell inside one (language, attribute, probe position) receives the same tie-break direction, so any position bias enters both sides of **every planned same-language, same-attribute, same-*k* cell-versus-cell contrast** identically and cancels in the difference — by construction rather than by arithmetic accident. Every contrast in this design is of that form: the confirmatory family tests cell A against cell C separately at each *k*, and the void-filling estimand is D minus E at matched *k*. The tie-break direction does vary across *k* and across language, so this cancellation would not extend to a cross-*k* or Chinese-versus-English inferential comparison; the design makes none. This matters: an earlier version of the rule did key on the cell, and while cells A and C happened to land on matching parity, **cells D and E landed on opposite parity at every probe position — and D minus E is the void-filling estimand (§4.3).** The flaw was found by a zero-context cold review before any confirmatory data was collected, and the runner now carries a hard assertion that fails the batch before it starts if any two cells within a group ever diverge again. The rule is fully deterministic and reproducible, and presentation order is recorded per conversation and reported as a covariate check (§4.1) regardless.
4. **2026-08-13 — operational machinery not covered by the frozen documents**, all disclosed here: a pre-flight reachability check (one call per family before the batch, unrecorded); a per-family circuit breaker; a resume rule under which a conversation that was attempted and failed is never retried while one never attempted is picked up; cost thresholds at which the run warns and at which it stops; and the **non-determinism check of §3.4** (six conversations run twice under identical frozen inputs, before the confirmatory batch, to establish that replication within a cell samples real variation). None of these is a frozen requirement; the resume rule is an equal-attempts principle, and the non-determinism check is a methods disclosure whose result is reported whichever way it had come out.
**Found during the sprint (2026-08-14), after the confirmatory batch ran:**

5. **The model identifier the API resolved is more specific than the one we pinned, and the screening batch did not record its own.** We requested `gpt-5.5`; every OpenAI response reported `gpt-5.5-2026-04-23`. That dated build string is recorded per call for the confirmatory batch and is published. **The screening batch of 2026-08-11 did not capture the responding model string** — its runner recorded only the identifier sent — so we cannot demonstrate that the screen ran on the same dated build. Anthropic and xAI returned the identifier unchanged, so the question does not arise for them.

6. **The multi-source class absorbs a large share of citations, by the frozen rule operating as written.** Of 1,530 citations, 427 (27.9%) classify as *multiple sources*; of those, 393 are system-prompt-and-own-turns collisions. The mechanism is mechanical and was not anticipated at the stated scale: a model that has restated its installed attribute in its own earlier turns produces a citation that shares ≥ N characters with both the system prompt and its own text, and the frozen waterfall assigns such a citation to the ambiguity class. **No rule was adjusted after seeing this.** The internal composition of the class is published so a reader can see what is in it, and the citation-source predictability denominator excludes it exactly as pre-registered.

7. **The English arm's verbatim-match class is structurally empty.** The frozen sentence-splitting rule lists `。！？!?；;` and newline, and contains no ASCII full stop. The English addenda replace only N and the activity mask, so the splitter carries over unchanged; an English "sentence unit" is therefore a whole conversational turn, and an exact match to a whole turn effectively cannot occur. Observed: 0 of 180 English citations classify as verbatim matches, against 23.9% in Chinese. **The rule was not changed** — altering a classification rule after seeing that it produced an empty cell is precisely what pre-registration forbids. The English degree dimension is consequently a partial-versus-none dichotomy, and is reported as such. The English arm is descriptive only and enters no statistical family.

8. **Pre-registered branches that fired, with no discretion exercised.** (a) The degradation ladder reached **L2**: L0 and L1 both triggered failure condition (iii), separation, at max |coefficient| 22.6 and 21.3 respectively; the reported statistics are therefore the per-*k* Fisher exact tests, as the frozen ladder prescribes. (b) The **E-cell offset is disabled**: cell E returned zero valid single choices across all 270 conversations, far below the frozen threshold of 5, so the A/B asymmetry is reported without default-preference correction and the D−E estimand degenerates to D's unadjusted adoption rate — a case the frozen analysis plan wrote out in advance. (c) One response was **unparseable** (1 of 1,530, an empty first line); it is reported as its own category and was not manually rescued.

9. **Operational machinery added on 2026-08-14 for the analysis, not covered by the frozen documents**: the classification script (`analyse.py`), the aggregation and statistics script (`tables.py`), and the power calculation (`power_mde.py`). All three are published. The classification script carries 26 self-tests that run before any data is read. Two hard-coded summary claims in the aggregation script were found to be false against the script's own computed tables and were replaced with computed values before any output was used.

10. **2026-08-16 — the frozen instruction-source word list is Chinese, and the annotation set is not.** The frozen codebook defines the disclosure annotation set as *all* flipped conversations, with no language restriction; 33 flips occurred (21 Chinese, 12 English), all annotated, the ≤ 50 cap never binding. The frozen word list that provides the mechanical comparator on the same texts has 21 entries, of which exactly one (`prompt`) is ASCII, so on the 12 English conversations it is nearly inert by construction — it fired 0 times there and once across the 21 Chinese. This is the same class of finding as item 7. **The rule was not changed**; §B6 reports the full-set rate (3.0%), the composition, and the Chinese-only rate (4.8%) side by side. Found by a cold review of post-coverage text on 16 August, before submission.
